Build a 32-bit adder by connecting two 16-bit adders. The provided add16 module adds two 16-bit numbers with a carry-in and produces a 16-bit sum and a carry-out. The top_module must add two 32-bit numbers a and b by splitting them into low and high 16-bit halves. The carry-out from the low half adder becomes the carry-in for the high half adder. The final carry-out from the high half is discarded. The 32-bit sum is the concatenation of the high and low sums.
